# Availability vs desired hours

Availability windows are a **hard constraint**. A staff member cannot be assigned outside them (recurring weekly windows plus one-off exceptions).

Desired weekly hours are a **fairness target only**. They never block an assignment. Managers will see under/over hours on the fairness report in a later phase.

Wall-clock windows such as 9:00–17:00 are interpreted in the **location timezone of the shift**, so the same “9–5” covers Pacific time at Cannon Beach and Eastern time at Charleston, including DST transitions.
