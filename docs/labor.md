# Overtime and consecutive days

- Weekly hours warn at **35+** and overtime cost is projected on hours over **40** at 1.5× wage.
- Daily hours warn above **8** and **hard-block above 12**.
- The 6th consecutive calendar day is a warning. The 7th requires a manager override with a documented reason.
- Any assignment that **starts** on a local calendar date counts as a worked day, whether it is 1 hour or 11 hours.
- The assign modal shows a **what-if** weekly/daily total before confirm.
